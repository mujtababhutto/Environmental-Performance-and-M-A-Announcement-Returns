Environmental Performance and M&A Announcement Returns

Do investors respond differently when an acquisition is announced by a company with stronger environmental performance?

This study examines US large-cap transactions announced between 2019 and early 2025. It connects acquirer environmental rankings and carbon intensity with short-term abnormal returns around each announcement.

Research question

Does a stronger environmental profile change the market’s first reaction to a deal?

An acquisition announcement reveals several things at once: the price being paid, the proposed financing, expected synergies and management’s confidence in the transaction. The study asks whether the acquirer’s environmental profile also helps explain the immediate share-price response.

The underlying intuition runs in both directions. Stronger environmental performance might signal better risk management and preparedness for regulatory change. It could also be secondary to the economics of the transaction, particularly within a short announcement window.

Data and approach

A deal-level event study

01

Transaction sample

US M&A announcements were combined with deal value, payment information and acquirer industry classifications.

02

Environmental measures

S&P environmental rankings and carbon emissions relative to sales were used to describe the acquirer’s environmental profile.

03

Announcement returns

Cumulative abnormal returns were examined over [-2,+2] and [-10,+10] windows around each announcement.

04

Cross-sectional analysis

The models relate announcement returns to environmental measures and deal size, with year effects, industry interactions and a post-SEC specification.

Results

The broad measures did not explain much of the immediate reaction

Environmental rank and carbon intensity were not statistically significant in the main regressions. The estimated relationship between deal size and acquirer returns was negative, while the year controls captured differences across announcement periods.

The main specifications did not show a clear relationship between the acquirer’s environmental profile and its short-term announcement return.

02

Industry context mattered

The paper reports a positive technology-sector interaction with environmental rank, a marginally negative interaction for financials and no significant energy-sector effect.

No post-proposal difference

The difference-in-differences model did not identify a significant change in the reaction to green acquirers following the SEC’s March 2022 climate-disclosure proposal.

Interpretation

Environmental performance may matter without becoming the announcement-day story

Environmental characteristics can shape long-term risk, regulation and corporate strategy. An acquisition announcement, however, concentrates attention on transaction-specific information. Price, financing, strategic fit and integration risk may dominate the initial response.

The findings also suggest that environmental information should be interpreted in context. The same measure can carry different implications across sectors, and a broad company-level score may not capture the environmental economics of a particular acquisition.

Practical implications

Corporate development

Connect environmental considerations to the economics of the transaction. A general sustainability profile is unlikely to replace a convincing explanation of price, strategy and execution.

Investors

Treat environmental ratings as part of the acquirer’s broader risk profile, not necessarily as a standalone short-term event signal.

Research

Transaction-specific environmental exposures and industry materiality may be more informative than a single aggregate score.

Scope and limitations

What the study can and cannot answer

The analysis focuses on short windows around M&A announcements. It does not measure whether environmentally stronger transactions create greater value over several years.

Environmental rankings and carbon intensity are broad company-level measures. They do not fully describe the environmental quality, liabilities or transition implications of each target and transaction.

M&A announcements also reveal several financially important facts at the same time. The estimates should therefore be read as evidence from this sample and design rather than a universal conclusion about ESG and acquisition value.
